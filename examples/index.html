Examples

  • Live GeoJSON: points, lines and shapes that update several times a second.
  • Your own basemap: a street map from a .pmtiles file made with maplet-tiles. It looks for data/seattle.pmtiles, or add ?tiles= and the path to your file.
  • Click and hover: buildings that light up under the pointer, popups for parks and places, and a pin you can drag. It uses the same data/seattle.pmtiles street map.
  • Heatmap: where shops and places crowd together in downtown Seattle, drawn from the points in the same street map file.
  • Day and night: the sun's light at any date and time, with night and twilight over the world and buildings lit from the sun. Add ?imagery= and a tile address to see it on your own satellite tiles.
  • Terrain: hills shaded and raised in 3D from an elevation .pmtiles or .mbtiles file. Open one with the button, or put it at data/terrain.pmtiles. Add ?tiles= with your street map file to see it on the hills.
