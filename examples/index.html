Examples

  • Live GeoJSON: points, lines and shapes that update several times a second.
  • Your own basemap: a street map from a .pmtiles file made with maplet-tiles. It looks for data/seattle.pmtiles, or add ?tiles= and the path to your file.
  • Terrain: hills shaded and raised in 3D from an elevation .pmtiles or .mbtiles file. Open one with the button, or put it at data/terrain.pmtiles. Add ?tiles= with your street map file to see it on the hills.
